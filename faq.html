FAQ

What is Sea Bandits?

Sea Bandits is a Free-to-Play Online Multiplayer High Seas Pirate RPG taking place in the vast player-driven ocean. Create an account, and venture adrift amongst other pirates alike, find treasures, complete quests, upgrade your ship and be social with other players. Defeat monsters, NPC Ships, and other players, or explore the world as a peaceful forager of goods, making a name for yourself as a feared pirate or a nice bloke who gets along with everyone. The game is meant to be socially focused, with opportunities to create or join a guild and engage in large sea battles.

Is Sea Banits PvP or PvE-oriented?

Sea Bandits is rich on both PvP and PvE. PvP zones are free-for-all PvP & PvE zones where you travel to collect the rarest loot, slay high level monsters or NPC Ships, or fight other players either solo or by teaming up with your guild / group of friends in fierce battles against other players. But be careful - there are pirates here who might not have any mercy that will do everything to sink your ship, much to the joy of your opponent. PvE zones are zero-risk zones - considered safe-havens. You can still slay monsters and have fun in these zones, but for those daring to engage in PvP, the rewards are plentiful, making the risk worth it.

Which weapon types are there in Sea Bandits?

There are two weapon types; cannons and harpoons. Cannons are used in battles against ships and harpoons are used to kill sea monsters.

Which types of ammunition exists?

There are several different levels of ammunition for the two weapon types, the higher the level of ammunition you have, naturally the higher your damage will be

Which types of ship upgrades exist?

There are various types of ship upgrades that you can upgrade your ship with: Slaves, Ship hull, and much more.

Which skills will Sea Bandits feature?

Progression in Sea Bandits is based on advancing your skills based on your play-style. Improving your skills allows you to do higher damage against either ships or monsters, depending on which skill you advance The following skill sheets are planned for Sea Bandits: Damage, Defense, Economy

What in-game currencies will Sea Bandits have?

There are two types of in-game currencies: 1. Gold (Can be obtained by completing quests, slaying sea monsters, sinking NPC Ships and engaging in PvP battles to board enemy players) 2. Pearls (Can be obtained by completing quests, from treasures on the ocean and by completing bonus maps)

Is Sea Bandits Pay to Win?

We will allow microtransactions to to a certain extent, but we never intentionally implement mechanics that makes it vastly superior to purchase in-game currency to advance much further than other players. Other than that, we monetize Sea Bandits through a optional premium subscription and cosmetic purchases, which unlocks non-pay-to-win convenience features (extra ship designs, exclusive titles, limited ship design cosmetic etc.).

How will the world look like?

The world of Sea Bandits is made of isometric tiles forming charted ocean maps connecting each other. The map charts form the vast ocean of Sea Bandits, and consists of 2 types of maps. PvE zones are mainly for new players, used strictly for PvE zones and are rich in loot, monsters, NPCs and quests etc. PvP zones are the most dangerous, and Sea Bandits consists mainly of these map charts. These maps are home to rare high-level monsters and high-level NPC Ships, but be careful; anyone you run into can attack you. It is also in these territories that you fight over control of the region, which reward you with a special bonuses on that map, and allows you to enter island safe-havens only accessible by you or your guild. Anyone can fight for control of the map, so watch out! Open-world bosses also spawn in both PvE and PvP zones. Get to them first and slay them for rare cosmetic rewards. Sea Bandits will also have bonus maps which can be completed after obtaining all the pieces of the lost map

What are cosmetic items?

In Sea Bandits, we collectively call all premium ship designs. “Cosmetics”. Cosmetic ship designs never have any impact on gameplay (aka, they do not make you stronger).

What are “collectibles”?

Some cosmetic items in Sea Bandits exist in a finite quantity. Every month, new cosmetics are introduced and sold through themed micro-transactions (or, occasionally, distributed through PvE), and after the month is gone, those cosmetics will never be re-introduced. That’s what makes collectibles; they are scarce, digital items and they all have an item history as well, detailing who has owned them, how they were first acquired, and more. This makes every item 100% unique. Each cosmetic item has its own story to tell! Players who wish to acquire a certain cosmetic that is no longer being distributed will have to buy them from other players for the game’s currency.

How can I get cosmetics?

The cosmetics of the month can be acquired through themed micro-transactions and PvE. Afterwards, you will have to buy them from other players for in-game currency. As an avid PvE player, this also means you get a chance to earn cosmetic items without ever having to spend any money yourself, that is by acquiring cosmetics through gameplay.

Do I need a beefy computer to play Sea Bandits?

No, you do not. Sea Bandits is a 2D game with no tech-savvy rendering, and with optimized code, we ensure that Sea Bandits can be played on all PC's.

When will Sea Bandits be released?

Official launch is scheduled before the end of 2024.

On which platforms will Sea Bandits release?

Sea Bandits will be released on PC, accessible from both Windows, Linux and Mac as a stand-alone client. After launch, we have plans to start working on implementations that allows for the game to be played in browsers.
